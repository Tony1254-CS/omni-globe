Based on the OmniSphere 2.0 spec and the Time Machine mega-request, three big feature areas are still incomplete, plus a short polish pass. Here's what I'll finish.

### 1. Global Time Machine v2 (finish Feature 1)
Currently only the timeline scrubber, GIBS layer, historical quakes, GDELT news and milestone narration exist. Still missing:

### 2. Causal Oracle AI (Feature 2)
